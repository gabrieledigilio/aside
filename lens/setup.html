Put Aside on your glasses

This page is for the Mac that runs Aside. Scan a code below with your iPhone's Camera app.

Checking the brain on this Mac…

A

Show the lens on the glasses

Adds Aside to Meta Ray-Ban Display as a web app. The lens is drawn by the glasses themselves.

Waiting for the outside link
  1. Scan the code. It opens the Meta AI app, which adds Aside to your glasses.
  2. On the glasses, open Aside from your apps.
  3. For sound, link the iPhone app with code B. Until then you can use “Test with this Mac’s mic” on the preview page.
B

Link the Aside iPhone app

Lets the iPhone app use this Mac’s model key, so you never type the key on the phone, and lets the phone send what the glasses hear to this Mac.

Waiting for the outside link
  1. Install the Aside app on your iPhone from Xcode first.
  2. Scan the code. It opens Aside and saves the link.
  3. In the app, choose where the thinking happens: on the phone or on this Mac.

The outside link is new every time Aside is restarted, so scan again after a restart. It works only while the Terminal window stays open, and this Mac stays awake while it does.

The link and its code are random. Someone who had both could read the lens while Aside runs, so do not post them. Aside keeps nothing on a server of its own: audio never leaves the phone, the words go to Meta’s model to be understood, and Aside drops them when the conversation ends.
